# 90–120-second recording script

Run `.\Start-Recording.cmd` for a fresh separate database; open http://127.0.0.1:8766. Never reset the preserved original. Rehearse once, stop that server, then launch another new recording session. Keep the notes in [hero_scenario.md](hero_scenario.md) ready to paste. Target 115–120 seconds; this is an unrecorded storyboard, not a claim of completed browser acceptance.

| Time | Screen / action | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10 s | Mission Control; synthetic watermark and KPIs | “ShopfloorIQ is my independent manufacturing analytics demonstration. The operation and every record are synthetic. I wanted to connect production data, trustworthy reporting and human investigation.” |
| 10–27 s | Open INC-1042; timeline and BUF-1042 | “This order has both a reporting gap and a real wait. Its missing completion makes it look 480 minutes old. This separate receipt supports 190 minutes elapsed and 720 completed units. The source references make that claim inspectable.” |
| 27–48 s | Human review; Alex acknowledges and proposes | “As the operator, I record what I checked and propose the evidence recovery. Nothing enters trusted reporting yet. An independent supervisor must review the receipt.” |
| 48–65 s | Switch Sam; approve, then resolve data incident | “Approval validates the source, updates the metrics and records the decision together. This is analytics evidence approval, not product release. The data incident can now close.” |
| 65–78 s | Mission Control; briefly open audit if time permits | “Trusted throughput rises from 86,880 to 87,600. Late-order share rises too: better evidence reveals the late order rather than making performance look better.” |
| 78–100 s | INC-1043 → Human review; acknowledge and record prepared next step with fill/pack references | “The 140-minute wait is a separate operational question. Its cause is still unknown. Sam owns the investigation and records the missing information and next evidence check. A plan is not a completed corrective action.” |
| 100–118 s | Mission Control → Shift handover; operational item first, expand held-order register | “The next shift receives the owner, status, evidence and next step, alongside every held order. Method Lab also challenges the detector with false alerts, missed delays and sparse data. The aim is a defensible decision, with clear limits.” |

Keep the final screen on the actionable handover. If the form entry exceeds two minutes, shorten the introduction and rehearse the prepared text; do not skip the independent approval or imply that investigation work has been completed. The Method Lab stress table is a useful follow-up screen after the main recording, rather than rushing a seventh module into the core story.

Do not claim deployment, affiliation, regulatory approval, verified root cause, product release, production accuracy or actual savings. Before final recording, complete the browser checklist in [ACCEPTANCE.md](../ACCEPTANCE.md). The updated interface has not completed visual acceptance.
